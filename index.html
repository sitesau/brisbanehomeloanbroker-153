Tailoring Questions by Borrower Type

First home buyers, refinancers, investors, and construction borrowers should approach their initial discussions with distinct sets of questions. For first home buyers, the source page indicates that specialist guidance, low deposit options, and Queensland government grants are key areas of support. Therefore, the primary question should revolve around eligibility checks and the available support mechanisms before selecting a lender.

Homeowners considering refinancing an older variable loan have a different set of priorities. The critical comparison involves not only the current interest rate but also any changes in fees, the loan's flexibility, and how well the updated loan aligns with current household financial needs. Investor loans typically require more structured planning from the outset. The investor example on the source page mentions accessing existing equity and structuring interest-only periods, suggesting that discussions should encompass existing property details, cash flow, and how new acquisitions integrate with current debt obligations. ASIC Moneysmart's guidance for property investment further advises borrowers to factor in potential vacancies, maintenance costs, and interest rate fluctuations.

For those undertaking construction projects, the source page notes the availability of progress payment lending from specialist brokers, referencing the Bracken Ridge knockdown rebuild scenario. This implies that questions about staged funding, necessary build documentation, and project timelines should be addressed before finalising any lender comparison.
